## A Belated Birthday Present

This visit doubles as a belated 4th birthday present for Kannon! He's excited to explore all the hands-on exhibits. As part of our new tradition, we're alternating years — parties one year, fun experiences like this trip the next. Hallie (18 months) has no clue what's coming, but we're hopeful there are plenty of toddler-friendly activities at Kaleideum for her to enjoy too.

## What is Kaleideum?

Kaleideum is a children's museum and science center located in downtown Winston-Salem, North Carolina. It combines the former Kaleideum North and Kaleideum South locations into one amazing educational destination for families.

## Our Experience

We had a great time at Kaleideum! Many fun exhibits and areas for both Kannon and Hallie. As you move up floors, things got progressively older or more complicated overall. The second floor had a lot of little fun things. The rooftop would have been a lot of fun but it rained and was very humid. Kannon slipped and had a little fall outside. We didn't spend too much time outside on the rooftop. Overall the kids enjoyed the third floor water experience the most.

We ate Jimmy John's before to just eat quickly and get to the museum. We had a great time and will likely go back again.
